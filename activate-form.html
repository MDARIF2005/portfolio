Activate the portfolio contact form

  1. Press the button below once. It sends a normal form post to FormSubmit.
  2. FormSubmit shows a page saying an activation email was sent.
  3. Open arifsmart2005@gmail.com (check spam too) and click Activate Form.
  4. After that, the form on your portfolio sends straight to your inbox.

You only need this page once. After activating, you can delete activate-form.html.
